# The AI Revolution of 2026: Are You Watching or Participating?

Let's talk a bit about how fast AI is changing business in 2026. Is it really just a bubble? Or as an individual, can you actually benefit from all of this? It's happening all around us right now.

Think about our generation—those of us born in 1988 or the 90s. During the 2000 dot-com bubble, we weren't really part of the situation. I was only 10 years old back then, so I didn't know much about what was happening in the tech world. But now, 25 years later, a massive revolution is unfolding, and this time we are part of it. We are witnessing every bit and piece of it.

Undoubtedly, this is "the big one." Just like the dot-com era changed the world's business, employment, and the global economy, we are seeing it happen again. I wouldn't say this just started in 2025; there have been many intelligent processes in the evolution of this tech. But there is always a tipping point, right? Once you hit that "100," that's when everyone finally sees it and talks about it.

This is that "100" moment. It starts with LLMs, and it is taking humanity into a new era of technology.

It really comes down to a choice for you: Will you sit in the backyard and watch this tech pass you by while thinking the "old school" way? Or will you be part of the revolution? If you jump in, you can derive the benefits for yourself and, more importantly, stay contemporary and relevant.

That is really what it's all about.
