# Hybrid learning rhythms for holistic schools

Modern schooling often fragments knowledge into 45-minute bells. How can we redesign the day to respect the child's natural learning rhythms?

## Integrating Systems

*   **Montessori**: Self-directed, hands-on learning blocks.
*   **CBSE/Standard**: Structured rigor and metrics.
*   **Gurukula**: Values, nature, and community living.

## The Proposed Rhythm

A day that breathes—alternating between deep focus work, community dialogue, and physical engagement with nature.
