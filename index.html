Our pillars

Three lenses for asking better questions about technology

The Tech Philosophers framework blends critical analysis, creative experimentation, and grassroots community insight. Choose a pillar to explore how we work.

Ethics Lab

We synthesize philosophy, law, and lived experience to prototype ethical guardrails for emerging technologies. Think practical frameworks, open-source guidelines, and scenario planning that keeps humans at the center.

  • Publish thought experiments to stress-test new products.
  • Partner with startups to co-create responsibility playbooks.
  • Host monthly roundtables with ethicists and policy makers.

Futures Studio

Our studio turns speculative ideas into tangible prototypes. We run design sprints, rapid experiments, and open labs to imagine how human values can inform intelligent systems.

  • Create immersive experiences that explore alternative futures.
  • Publish quarterly field guides on emerging technologies.
  • Mentor founders building humane tools and civic tech.

Community Voices

We highlight artists, educators, and activists shaping our tech culture. Through editorial features and open salons we build inclusive spaces for nuance and honest debate.

  • Curate multimedia stories amplifying global perspectives.
  • Hold neighborhood listening tours and youth workshops.
  • Maintain a community knowledge base that anyone can remix.
