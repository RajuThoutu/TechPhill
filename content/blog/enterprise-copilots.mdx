# Designing enterprise copilots that respect human judgment

As enterprises rush to adopt AI copilots, a critical question emerges: how do we design systems that augment rather than replace human judgment?

## The Trust Gap

Current implementations often focus on efficiency rather than efficacy. A copilot that automates a decision without explaining the 'why' is a black box that erodes trust.

## Guardrails & Governance

We need architectural guardrails that:
1.  **Verify**: Ensure output matches policy.
2.  **Explain**: Provide citations and reasoning.
3.  **Defer**: Hand over to humans when confidence is low.
