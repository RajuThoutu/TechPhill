# Why Krishna intervened before Kamsa’s downfall

Krishna's intervention wasn't just about ending tyranny; it was about the timing of the intervention. 

## Risk Sensing

Leaders must sense the tipping point—when a systemic issue (Kamsa) goes from being a localized problem to an existential threat.

## Moral Calculus

The decision to act requires balancing immediate disruption against long-term stability. Krishna's actions teach us about strategic patience and decisive strikes.
