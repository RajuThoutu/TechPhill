In an age where AI can produce output instantly, the value of *transactional* work creates a commodity trap. The only non-commoditized asset remains deep human connection.

## The Gen Z Paradox

Growing up hyper-connected yet isolated, the leaders of tomorrow are seeking meaning over metrics.

## Purposeful Relationships

Building networks based on shared values (Dharma) rather than just shared interests (Kama) or goals (Artha) creates resilience in volatile times.
